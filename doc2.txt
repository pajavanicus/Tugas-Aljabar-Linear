McLaren showcased exceptional pace and strategic brilliance at the Miami Grand Prix, resulting in a phenomenal double podium finish for the team. Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri crossed the finish line in second and third place, respectively, firmly establishing McLaren as a primary challenger for the constructors' title. The team's recent aerodynamic upgrades proved highly effective on the demanding street circuit.

Norris had already set the tone for a successful weekend by dominating the Sprint race on Saturday. Starting from pole position in the shortened format, the British driver controlled the race perfectly to take the sprint victory. This early success boosted the team's morale and provided valuable data regarding tyre degradation and race pace in the hot and humid Florida conditions.

During the main Grand Prix on Sunday, both McLaren drivers executed their race strategies with precision. Despite Norris's admission that a slightly earlier pit stop to avoid the undercut might have given him a chance to challenge for the win, securing two spots on the podium was celebrated as a massive achievement. Piastri's consistent lap times and defensive driving secured his third-place finish, completing a highly successful transatlantic trip for the Woking-based squad.
