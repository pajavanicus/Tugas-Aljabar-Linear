Max Verstappen experienced a highly eventful and challenging race at the Miami Grand Prix, ultimately crossing the finish line in fifth position. The reigning champion's race was compromised right from the beginning when he suffered a dramatic 360-degree spin at Turn 1 on the opening lap. After locking his brakes and squeezing his competitors, the Red Bull driver momentarily lost control but miraculously kept his car out of the barriers and continued the race.

Despite the early setback, Verstappen demonstrated his characteristic resilience and fighting spirit. He embarked on a spectacular recovery drive, carving his way through the field with a series of bold overtaking maneuvers. The deployment of a Safety Car later in the race provided an opportunity for strategic variation, allowing Verstappen to briefly inherit the lead as other drivers pitted for fresh rubber.

However, the strategic gamble meant Verstappen found himself defending against cars on much fresher and softer tyres during the final stint of the race. Running on heavily worn Hard compound tyres, he was eventually passed by the leading trio. Nevertheless, his fierce defense and subsequent battle with Charles Leclerc in the closing laps provided some of the most thrilling wheel-to-wheel racing of the entire weekend.
