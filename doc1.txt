The 2026 Miami Grand Prix delivered a spectacular weekend of racing, culminating in a historic victory for Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli. Withstanding immense pressure from the chasing pack, Antonelli secured his third consecutive Formula 1 victory. The young driver showed remarkable composure throughout the 57-lap race around the Miami International Autodrome, managing his tyres effectively while defending his lead against relentless attacks from his rivals.

Antonelli made history during this event by becoming the first driver in the sport's history to convert their maiden three pole positions into race wins. Starting from the front of the grid, he maintained his advantage through the tricky opening sequence of corners and dictated the pace for the majority of the afternoon. This achievement solidified his status as a formidable contender in the current era of Formula 1.

The victory in Miami also had significant implications for the World Championship standings. By claiming the maximum points available on Sunday, Antonelli extended his lead over his teammate George Russell and the rest of the field. The Mercedes team's strategic execution, combined with Antonelli's flawless driving, demonstrated their absolute dominance during this phase of the season.
